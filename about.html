About Arcifact

Arcifact Ltd is an independent research company registered in England. We build instruments that compute exact answers about systems defined by constraints, and certificates designed to make those answers independently verifiable.

The work is inspectable. The Oracle computes the exact admissible orderings of a real 24-job CI workflow live in the browser. Published evaluation results can be checked here, and the open Arcifact Kit provides frozen instruments, scoring tools and lightweight verification code for independent reproduction.

Every published result carries its provenance. Preregistered failures remain in the record. Two UK patent applications, GB2618664.3 and GB2619009.0, cover methods developed through this work.

Arcifact was founded by Ben Duncan, sole inventor on both applications and author of its instruments and published research. If you write to ben@arcifact.io, the person who built the measurement answers.

If you run pipelines, contracts or other systems where order, constraints and evidence matter, the pilot starts with a 30-minute cold read on one of your own files.

You keep the certificate.
