Machine-readable schema: certificate.schema.v1.json. A certificate is a self-sealed statement by Arcifact Ltd. The public verifier in the kit (tools/verify_certificate.py) checks schema, self-seal, bank digests, manifest root and revocation offline, without contacting us. Issued certificates carry an Ed25519 signature verifiable against Arcifact's independently published issuer key. Unsigned documents are reports, not issued certificates.

What it is. What it is not.

It is
A proprietary evidence statement: measured conduct on frozen instruments, in a declared envelope, against bars set before the runs, renewable per release, revocable in public.
It is not
A government accreditation, a legal warranty, or a claim about conduct outside the declared envelope. Anyone selling you the latter is selling something no measurement supports.
Independence
Arcifact certifies systems it did not build. For our own governed configuration, the same instruments, manifests and bars are published so the referee is you.
Renewal
A certificate names the release it measured. New release, new run, new certificate; expiry is automatic at twelve months regardless.
Corrections
Errors found in issued certificates are corrected and listed at arcifact.io/verify; disputes go to legal@arcifact.io and are answered by the founder in writing.
