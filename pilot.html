Certification

The pilot.

Three weeks, one system, bars agreed in writing before anything is scored. At the end you hold a certificate that survives hostile reading, or a miss report worth more than most passes.

Start the conversation See the evidence first
// § 01 · the shape

Week by week, nothing hidden.

W1 Your run You run the public kit against your endpoint, on your infrastructure. Nothing leaves your machines. We agree thresholds in writing: what counts as a pass, before any pass is possible.
W2 Scored runs Frozen banks, including held-out items never published anywhere. Your system, measured where no preparation is possible.
W3 Certificate Banded scores, explicit envelope, stance decomposition, fabrication rate, provenance on every number, and a joint read-out with the founder.
// § 02 · the terms

Fixed fee. Your approval controls publication.

The fee is fixed and agreed with the bars, so scope cannot creep and neither can the standard. Certificates publish only with your approval; misses stay private unless you choose otherwise, and a missed bar is reported with a mechanism analysis, never repositioned. Certification renews per release, so the claim tracks your product rather than a moment it once had.

// § 02b · fit

Good fit, poor fit, said plainly.

Good fit
You ship an assistant or agent whose fabricated citations, invented policies or misreported actions carry real cost, and you can expose an endpoint or responses files within a week.
Poor fit
You want a benchmark victory lap, a guarantee outside a declared envelope, or a certificate without a measurement. We will decline politely and quickly.
You receive
The certificate (sample and schema), its manifests on the public verify page, the joint read-out, and renewal terms in writing.
// § 03 · what we need

An endpoint, a contact, a decision.

An API endpoint or responses files. One technical contact who can answer questions about regimes and release cadence. A decision on publication, which you can change later in either direction. That is the whole list. You will be dealing with the founder throughout, and the first move is yours to make alone: the kit is public, and your first number costs a minute.
