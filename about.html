About Arcifact.

Arcifact Ltd is an independent research company registered in England. It builds instruments that compute exact answers about systems defined by constraints, and certificates that let the recipient check every claim without trusting the sender.

What exists today is on this site and in the open kit. The Oracle runs live here, computing the exact space of admissible orderings for a real twenty-four job CI workflow. The verification page checks published results in the browser. The kit carries the forty-line light verifier and the certificate schema, so a claim made here can be checked on your machine, offline. Two UK patent applications, GB2618664.3 and GB2619009.0, name the methods. Every number published on these pages carries its provenance, and results that missed their preregistered bars are kept in the record, not deleted from it.

The company is founded and run by Ben Duncan, sole inventor on both applications and author of every instrument and every published figure. Write to ben@arcifact.io and the person who built the measurement answers for it.

Earlier public work, a persistent Bayesian research engine and a chess demonstration built on it, remains available and now leads here; this site is the product.

If you run pipelines, contracts, or any system where order and evidence matter, the pilot page describes a thirty-minute cold read on a file of yours. You keep the certificate.
