// § 04 · governed agents

It hunts the missing fact.

Faced with an open question, the agent neither guesses nor stalls. It names the observation with the most settling power, performs it, and repeats until the evidence closes, then answers with receipts. Where it cannot close, it says so, shortfall named. On a corpus it had never seen: 270 episodes, zero unwarranted assertions, observation counts at or under the computed optimum.

Running coding or workflow agents? An agent that misreports what it did is the exact class this closes.

// § 06 · method

I publish what failed.

Every number on this page was scored against a threshold set before its run, and the record keeps the failures beside the passes. Banks ship only past screens for surface shortcuts, re-rendering and positional strategies, with chance floors stated. The policy is public in the protocol. Verification is public. Manufacture is not.
