Policy

Security disclosure.

Found something wrong in our instruments, our scorer, our site or our conduct? Tell us directly and we will deal with it directly.

// § 01 · reporting

Write to security@arcifact.io.

Include what you found, where, and how to reproduce it. For scorer or bank issues, attach the output of tools/verify_hashes.py and a minimal responses file. We acknowledge within three working days and keep you informed until resolution. A machine-readable copy of this policy lives at /.well-known/security.txt.

// § 02 · good faith

Test honestly and we will treat you honestly.

Good-faith research against our published surfaces is welcome. Do not access data that is not yours, degrade service for others, or publish before we have had a fair chance to fix. We do not run a bounty programme yet; we do credit reporters who want credit.
