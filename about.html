About Arcifact.

One company, one thesis: machine judgment you can verify. Everything we ship is a form of that sentence.

// § 01 · the line

Three projects, one architecture.

Arcifact began with verifiable computation: Persistent Bayesian State, a research engine whose posterior computations become content-addressed, hash-chained, exactly re-queryable artifacts, with thirteen machine-checked theorems behind the exactness claims. A public chess build demonstrated the substrate. The Evidence Certificate is the product built on the same conviction: results that carry their own receipts. If you followed an old link and found Bayesian tooling or chess, you found the substrate and the demonstration; this site is the product.

// § 02 · the founder

Built and answered for by one person.

Arcifact Ltd is an independent research company registered in England, founded by Ben Duncan, who is the sole inventor on UK patent application GB2618664.3 and the author of the instruments, the protocol and every published number. When you write to ben@arcifact.io, the person who built the measurement answers for it. That is a small company's disadvantage turned into the only guarantee that scales: nothing on this site is somebody else's claim.
